REPORT STRUCTURE
This structure replaces the generic Summary, Key Takeaways, and Conclusion layout for this task. Write in Markdown with exactly these second-level headings, in this order, and no introduction before the first heading.

## Overall Evaluation
The essay type; the prompt or context status; word count against the limit (flag any overage or large shortfall); two to four sentences on what the essay communicates; and one sentence on confidence and limitations, including any missing reviewer.

## Scores at a Glance
A compact table with the columns Reviewer, Native score, Reference score (0 to 100), and Note.

## What the Reviewers Found
Shared findings first, each labeled Consensus, Majority, Single reviewer, or Conflicted as the Engine defines them, with the reviewers named. Then the points where reviewers differ, resolved using the Engine's conflict resolution order, with the tradeoff explained. If Reviewer 3 raises an authenticity concern about another reviewer's suggestion, adjudicate it explicitly. Preserve a substantiated minority finding. Do not treat agreement as proof.

## Three Greatest Strengths
Exactly three. Each refers to something concrete the reviewers pointed to in the essay.

## Three Highest-Impact Improvements
Exactly three, ranked using the Engine's priority order. Each states what to improve, why it matters, where it appears (using words a reviewer quoted), which reviewers raised it, the revision strategy, and what the student should preserve. Distinguish structural changes from sentence-level polish.

## Reviewer Perspectives
One or two lines per reviewer, naming it and giving its native score where it has one.

## Suggested Revision Sequence
A short numbered action plan, in the order the student should work.

## Reflection Questions
Up to three questions drawn from the reviewers' questions or your synthesis, specific to this essay.

## Writing Integrity Reminder
One or two sentences: the essay must stay the student's own words and experiences, and any example wording from the reviewers is for inspiration only and must not be submitted as original experience.
